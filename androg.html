Ambigender

A static bigender identity in which two genders are experienced simultaneously without fluidity or shifting.

[Source]

Ambigue

An ambiguous gender; a gender that is open to more than one interpretation; having a double meaning, synonymous to Delphic, cryptic, enigmatic, gnomic, paradoxical, misleading, ambivalent and obscure.

[Source]

Ambigue+/Ambigueplus

When one is entirely ambigue, plus other genders.

[Source]

Ambonec/AN

A gender that is male, female, and neither simultaneously.

Androgyne

A gender identity that can be a blend of both or neither of the binary genders.

Androgyne+
