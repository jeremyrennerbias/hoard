Specific identities

Alterhuman

Alterhumans are individuals who have identities beyond what is traditionally considered human. An alterhuman can identify as non-human, or they can identify as being human in ways alternate to what is societally common.

[Source]

Otherhearted

A person who has a strong fundamental connection to a species or character which impacts their core sense of self. This is most commonly described in terms of identifying with a being as opposed to identifying as one, although this is not always the case.

[Source]

Therianthrope

An individual who identifies either as an animal species that exists or has existed on Earth, or as a non-human species which experiences particularly animalistic instincts or urges.

[Source]

Transspecies

A word used by some alterhumans to describe a specific relationship to nonhumanity. It is typically used to draw parallels between the experiences of species identity and gender identity.

[Source]

Fictionkin

People who identify as a fictional character or species. The object of one's identification is called their fictotype. Generally, being fictionkin is involuntary, long-term, and intrinsic to one's selfhood.

[Source]

Human non-conforming (HNC)

An umbrella label encompassing all identities and subcultures that somehow incorporate nonhuman elements in any way.

[Source]

Queerhuman

A label under the alterhuman umbrella (like alter-/non-/overhuman) that describes a ‘queer’ experience with alterhumanity. One may feel like their alterhuman identity is 'queer’, viewed through a queer lense, linked in with queerness, and/or 'queer’ instead of being strictly alter-, non-, overhuman etc.

[Source]

Humanqueer

A term for people who experience humanity or relate to humanity in a non-normative or queer way.

[Source]

Overhuman/Humanmore

An alternative term to alterhuman that has a different meaning when describing your general alterhuman status. Humanmore is meant to be a general umbrella term, so there are several experiences that fall under it. Overhuman's core experience is being human, but also alterhuman. The user still considers themself human in some way, and finds importance with both their humanity and their alterhumanity.

[Source]

Species Non-Conforming (SNC)

Denoting or relating to a person whose behavior or appearance does not conform to prevailing cultural and social expectations about what is appropriate to their species.

[Source]

Intrashifter

One who can metaphysically and or physically transform internally in some sense. This may refer to literal internal change, like hormones, bones or organs, or non-literal changes like headspace and behaviour.

[Source]

Soulshifter

A shapeshifter who can't voluntarily shift; rather, they take the form of whatever they perceive themselves as at any given moment.

[Source]

Novellhuman

Someone who is non-human, who desires or craves humanity, and is living in a human body.

[Source]
