Questions and Answers

Q: Is this hoard finished?

A: If you're reading this, then no, it is not finished.

Q: What is all of this? Do you really identify with all of these? Why???

A: These are all terms and flags I use to describe my identity; yes, I really do identify with all of them; and I do so because I enjoy having a smorgasboard of labels which describe me.

Having a lot of terms I identify with helps me understand all of the facets of my identity. I'm a big fan of knowing myself.

Q: Are you going to source who coined all of these terms?

A: Whenever I add a term, I also add the source. However, I sometimes cannot find the source, in which case I'll specify that it doesn't have a source.

Q: How accessible is this site?

A: Kind of accessible. As far as I know, all of the text on the website is perfectly screen-readable, I've used high (but not too high) contrast for the colour scheme of the site, and I've made the text and images large enough for me to read without my glasses on. However, I do need to add image ID to the flags on here.

Q: What does "SOGI" mean?

A: "Sexuality and gender identity". I use it to refer to my orientation and gender identity.

Overarching identities

Movements/subcultures (DONE!)

Terms which denote one's stance on something or one's political/social attitudes. -Punk, -queer, etc.

Combo flags (DONE!)

Terms which are the combination of two or more other terms. These are mostly here because I like accumulating flags.

Reclaimed terms (DONE!)

Slurs or other pejoratives used as identity.

Hoarding/collecting (DONE!)

Terms which describe how one collects or hoards identities.

Presentation (DONE!)

Terms which describe how one presents themselves, or an aspect of their identity.

Lighthearted/silly labels (DONE!)

Terms or flags which are more silly, unserious, or jokey in nature. Ex: a "wet soggy cat" flag.

Sonance

Terms which describe the dissonance between one’s identity, and how they are externally perceived or labelled (either by others or themselves).

Neoumbrellas (DONE!)

Broad umbrella terms which describe the traits of one's identity.

Non-SOGI identity terms

Umbrella terms

Terms which denote the traits of one's entire identity.

Soul terms

Terms which denote the traits of one's soul, essence, core, or any other abstract spiritual quality.

Mentality terms

Terms which denote the traits of one's mentality, emotions, or personality. Mad pride terms go here.

Body terms (DONE!)

Terms which denote the traits of one's physical body.

Alterhuman terms (DONE!)

Terms which, broadly, describe one's species.

Sex, gender, and orientation

Sex

Terms which describe sex identity. "Sex identity", for me, encompasses one's genitalia, hormones, and sex designation (AGAB).

Modalities and modal qualities

Terms which, broadly and generally, denote one's correspondence or lack of correspondence with the gender they were coercively assigned by their legal system at birth (CAGAB). Cis-, trans-, etc.

Protogenders and anthrogenders

Genders that are not neobinarine or xenine.

Neogenders

Genders that are neobinarine, including xenine/xenic genders. Dreadfully unsorted...

Orientation

Terms pertaining to attraction, orientation, and other things centering it.
