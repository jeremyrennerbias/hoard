Terms by themes of hoarded terms

A term for someone who hoards labels out of spite - specifically, hoarding identities (usually MOGAI ones) because the coiner has you on their DNI, and said they "don't want people on their DNI to use their terms".

Terms by nature of hoarding

Hyperqueer/Hyperlabeled

A term for someone who uses multiple microlabels (or, perhaps, hoards them) in a way that makes them feel very queer.

[Source]

Queleacollecter/Queleahoarder

A term for someone who constantly hoards new terms, even if they "barely fit" due to the size of someone's identity collection.

[Source]

Stargenderhoarder/Stargendervast

An identity where someone collects genders because their gender(s) cannot be defined no matter how many other terms are coined.

[Source]

Xenohoardcopeic

A term for those who hoard/collect xenogenders to cope/as a coping mechanism.

[Source]

Alphabethoardic

A hoarding label tied to hoarding the entire Latin/Roman alphabet in genders -- having a gender for every letter, A through Z.

[Source]

Label Cocktail

A term for those who feel like their identity is a "cocktail" of many labels.

[Source]

Attraset

A collector identity where an individual has multiple sets of attraction and identity terms that go together, or possibly even contradict eachother, and often fluctuate between these terms.

[Source]

Axenlector

A term for someone who hoards many xenogenders to try and understand themself better, but end up confusing themselves, which leads to them adding more.

[Source]

Chaofilecurator

A gender that feels like a crowded desktop with too many files on it, but also feels that the specific act of choosing, curating, and organizing genders is an important aspect of one's gender.

[Source]

Corvidcollector

A term for someone who collects terms like how a crow/raven collects trinkets.

[Source]

Crystalcollector

A term for someone who collects genders like pretty crystals.

[Source]

Gender Inventory

A gender hoarding term where the collected terms are sorted in an “inventory”. The inventory could “expand” to fit all hoarded genders (a ‘creative’ inventory) or has a static size and the hoarder has to give up labels once the capacity is reached (a ‘survival’ inventory.)

[Source]

Gendergreed

A term for someone who collects terms due to just wanting to, not neccesarily because they need/have to, with an inherent connection to the deadly sin of greed.

[Source]

Hoarderflux

A gender that oscillates between one's gender hoard, and being agender.

[Source]

Iocuscollector

A term for someone who collects labels or terms more for the fun of it than anything else.

[Source]

Magpiehoarder

A term for those who hoard genders they find "pretty" or "pleasing."

[Source]

Permahoarder/Permacollector

A term for those who hoard labels and do not drop any of them, even if they no longer apply to them; their labels are permanent.

[Source]

Digiorganizeaic

A term for those who feel as if they are tucking their hoarded genders into digital folders, and sorting them neatly for later use.

[Source]

Pokedexhoarder/Pokedexcollector

A term for those who feel as if their term hoard is like a Pokedex, and every new term they put into it is a new Pokemon they discovered.

[Source]

Overwhelmed Hoarder

A term for someone who hoards many genders, with the intention of making their hoard overwhelmingly long.

[Source]

Prismahoarder

A term for someone who feels as if their identity, upon closer look, "refracts" into a broad spectrum of identities, like a prism refracting one beam of light into a rainbow.

[Source]

Sweethoarder/Sweetcollector

A term for someone who constantly craves new genders or pronouns, like how one would crave candy if they had a sweet tooth.
